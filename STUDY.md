# Preliminary usability study

Owner-reported aggregate findings, supplied September 21, 2026. This summary is not an independently audited study; participant-level records and session dates are not included.

## Research question

Can assisted planning reduce the time students spend discovering, selecting, and scheduling courses?

## Participants and tasks

The project owner reports that **20 students used CourseWise**, comparing manual and assisted workflows involving:

1. Finding courses.
2. Assessing relevance to skills they wanted to develop.
3. Selecting courses.
4. Creating a schedule.

## Reported results

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | 20 students |
| Mean manual completion time | 8 minutes 20 seconds (500 seconds) |
| Mean assisted completion time | 5 minutes 50 seconds (350 seconds) |
| Difference between means | 2 minutes 30 seconds (150 seconds) |
| Relative reduction in mean time | (500 − 350) / 500 × 100 = 30% |

The result describes **30% lower average workflow completion time**, not a per-task improvement or a separately measured time to a conflict-free schedule.

## Limitations

- Recruitment, session dates, task ordering, and counterbalancing are not documented.
- Participant-level timings, variability, completion rates, and independent success assessments are unavailable.
- Practice effects may influence the comparison; the aggregate results do not establish causality or generalize to the wider student population.
- Skill relevance was part of the reported task, not a validated measure of learning or skill development.
- The summary does not establish separate interviews or attribute specific product changes to participant feedback.

## Next evaluation

Use equivalent tasks with balanced manual/assisted order. Record individual timings, successful completion, schedule validity, corrections, and unintended changes. Report variability alongside mean completion time, and distinguish speed from task quality.

Functional regression checks are separate from these usability findings.
